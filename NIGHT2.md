### 4. Kid safety: chat filter, reports, and a reports inbox for you
- **Chat filter (new `src/filter.js`)** in every chat: room chat, lobby chat, private chats and group chats.
  - **Rude words become `****`.** It also catches capital letters, look-alike numbers and symbols (1 for i, @ for a, $ for s), stretched letters ("soooo"), spaced-out spellings ("b a d") and mean phrases.
  - **Normal words are left alone:** class, cocktail, assembly, Dickens and night all pass.
  - **Personal info is hidden:** phone numbers become **[number hidden]** and emails become **[email hidden]**.
  - **Filtered when shown, not just when sent,** so a message from an old or modified copy of the game is still cleaned.
  - **Encoded word list:** stored encoded, so kids reading the code don't see the words. The file explains how to edit it.
- **Names:**
  - **Every player name** on screen is cleaned too (in the lobby, game, results and panels).
  - **Your own rude name** is swapped for "player" before anyone sees it, with a message asking for a kinder name.
  - **Refused outright:** rude account names and group names.
- **🚩 Report** button on every player's profile.
  - **The form:** pick a reason (mean or bullying, bad words, asking for personal info, cheating, rude name, something else) and add an optional note. If you were in a private chat with them, their last few messages are attached.
  - **Afterwards:** "Thanks for telling us", plus a one-click **Block them too**.
  - **Privacy and limits:** only the owner sees reports, and the reported player isn't told who sent it. One report every 30 seconds per player, enforced by the database rules, so nobody can flood your inbox.
- **Your reports inbox (owner only):** a red count on the 👑 button and a pop-up when a new report arrives.
  - In Owner tools → **Reports**: each report shows who, why, the note, who reported it and when.
  - **Ban 1 day** bans them straight from the report; **Dismiss** or **Done** clears it.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit tests (66 passing):**
    - **Filter:** catches the tricky spellings, leaves 10 normal phrases alone, hides phones and emails, and the source file contains no rude words.
    - **Report rules:** filing a report needs the rate-limit stamp; there's one every 30 seconds; nobody can report as someone else; only the listed reasons are accepted; notes are capped; and reports can't be overwritten. Banned players can't report. Only the owner can read or clear reports.
    - The rules simulator learned multi-path updates and a moving clock to test this.
  - **Browser test** with 3 tabs:
    - **Filter:** a rude word and a phone number in lobby chat arrive as `oh **** call me [number hidden]`. A rude name shows to others as "player", with a message to the player.
    - **Reporting:** Sam reports Tess for bad words with a note. An immediate second report is refused with "wait a little".
    - **Owner inbox:** the owner's 👑 shows "1" and the report lists Tess, Bad words, the note and Sam. Banning from the report works, then Dismiss clears it. Other players have no inbox. No errors.
- **Rules:** new `reports` and `reportsBy` sections (in the combined update at the bottom).
- **Test fix:** the Letter Lock browser check failed now and then. It looked from outside the page for the right letter on the line, so on a busy machine it could miss it, and it never confirmed a press worked. Now the page itself watches every frame for the letter, and each lock is confirmed (with a retry after a miss). The game itself was fine: the check's new diagnostics showed 58 to 60 frames per second and correct keyboard focus.

### 7. Background music
- **New `src/music.js`:** synthwave-style music made live by the browser, with no audio files to download, so it works offline too. Three tracks in A minor:
  - **Menu (84 BPM):** a soft pad with a gentle arpeggio. No drums.
  - **In game (112 BPM):** pad, a 16th-note arpeggio, bass, kick and hi-hats.
  - **Last 30 seconds (136 BPM):** faster bass, a kick on every beat, and a tense E major chord. It switches on by itself when a round has 30 seconds left, and back to the menu track after.
- **Volume:** a separate **Music** slider in **Settings → Sound**, next to Volume. Dragging it to 0 turns music off, and 🔇 Mute silences music too. It's quiet by default (30%), so it's easy on a Chromebook in class.
- **Starts with your first click or key press,** because browsers don't allow sound before that.
- **Built to be gentle on Chromebooks:** it shares the sound effects' audio engine and schedules notes slightly ahead of time, so it stays smooth even when the game is busy. A soft filter keeps it behind the sound effects.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit tests (70 passing):** the menu is slower than the game, which is slower than tense. Chords are right (A minor, F major, E major), and every note over 8 bars of every track is in key, the same every time, and uses that track's instruments. No drums on the menu and more when tense. The right track for each moment (including modes without a time limit). The music setting is checked and clamped like the volume.
  - **Browser test:**
    - Silent until the first click, then the menu track plays with notes coming.
    - The Music slider at 0 stops it and 30% brings it back; Mute silences it.
    - In a practice game the game track plays, then with 20 seconds left the tense track takes over, and leaving goes back to the menu track. No errors.
- **Rules:** no change needed.

### 8. King of the Hill (new mode)
- **New mode (`src/modes/koth.js`):** 2 to 8 players, bots allowed, 4-minute rounds. It's in the lobby after Co-op Heist, with its own mountain-and-flag icon and pink-purple color.
- **How it plays:**
  - **THE HILL** sits in the middle, sealed by a lock that every player sees the same way. The first to type the answer **captures the hill**, and a new lock goes on.
  - **Four kinds of locks,** quick to solve and kid-friendly: math ("PIN = 49 + 16"), a backwards word, a word shifted by 1 or 2 letters, and scrambled letters. Each has a hint.
  - **Holding the hill** scores every second you're on top. Type **fortify** to block captures for 10 seconds (it recharges in 30).
  - **A wrong answer jams your lock for 2 seconds,** so guessing numbers doesn't work.
  - **Most time on top wins.** The results show time held and captures.
- **Fair online:**
  - The host decides who captured first, by the order the answers arrived, and checks each answer against the lock. Late answers for an old lock don't count.
  - The host keeps its own up-to-date copy of the hill, so a slow connection can't cause a double capture.
- **Bots** crack locks at a speed set by the bot level (easy 14–26 s, normal 8–16 s, hard 4.5–9 s) and fortify now and then.
- **Also added:**
  - **Quick-chat:** "Lock is …", "Fortify now! 🛡", "Coming for the hill 😎" and "Get them! ⚔️".
  - **Spectators** see each player's time on top, with a 👑 for the holder.
  - **New Explorer trophies:** "King of the Hill Champ", "Regular" and "Expert", now 138 trophies in total.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit tests (71 passing):**
    - **Locks:** 480 of them (60 seeds × 8 captures) are the same for everyone, use all 4 kinds, and each can be worked out from its prompt. Math is right, scrambles use the same letters and are never already solved, and answers ignore capitals and spaces.
    - **The host's rules:** the right answer captures and credits the old holder's time. Too late, wrong answers, capturing your own hill, and capturing while fortified are all refused. Only the holder can fortify, and fortify has a cooldown. Time held counts the current holder's time.
  - **Browser test:**
    - **Two players:** a wrong answer jams; Kai captures the hill (a new lock goes on and Kit is told); Kai fortifies and Kit's answer is blocked. When the shield drops, Kit takes the hill and Kai keeps 10.5 s of credit. When time's up, "kai is King of the Hill!".
    - **Practice:** a hard bot captures the hill. No errors.
- **Rules:** no change needed (it uses the same room data as the other modes).
