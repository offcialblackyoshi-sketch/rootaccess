### 2. Group chats
- **New "Groups" section** at the top of the 👥 online players panel, with **+ new** to make a group (name up to 24 characters).
- **Inviting:** a new group opens with its member list, which shows everyone online you can invite. People who turned private messages off, or whom you blocked, aren't listed.
- **Invites need a yes.** The other player sees "📨 gina invited you to Byte Squad" with **Join** and **No thanks**. Nobody is ever put into a group without agreeing. Invites from players you've blocked are hidden.
- **Group chat window** (bottom right): messages, member count, and a **members** button.
  - The person who made the group can **remove** members and **delete** the group. Everyone else can **leave**. Both ask twice.
  - Removed players' window closes, with a notice.
- **Unread:** groups with new messages get a red dot, and count toward the 👥 badge and its sound.
- **Accounts and guests:**
  - Signed-in players join as their account, so their groups and history stay on every device.
  - Guests join as their tab, so their groups end when they close it. The panel says so.
  - Up to 12 people per group; the last 100 messages are shown.
- **Safety:** messages from blocked players are hidden. Banned players can't make groups, invite, or post (checked by the rules). The owner name stays reserved in group and member names.
- **3 new trophies:** Squad Up (make a group), Plus One (join someone's group), Group Text (send a group message). Now 133 in total.
- **New test tool, `tests/rules-sim.mjs`:** a small stand-in for the Firebase rules engine. It runs the real `database.rules.json` against a pretend database, so the rules are tested for real without the emulator.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit tests (62 passing):** about 40 permission checks against the real rules. Only you can make a group; joining needs a member's invite plus your own yes; no self-invites or fake re-invites; only members read or post, as themselves; banned players can't post; only the maker removes people or deletes the group; 12 is the limit; a deleted group can't be brought back by an old invite. Also inbox sorting and unread counting, and who can be invited.
  - **Browser test** with 3 tabs: Gina makes "Byte Squad" and invites Hank and Kim. Kim says no thanks and sees nothing; Hank joins. They chat both ways, and unread shows and clears. The 3 trophies unlock. Gina removes Hank (Hank can't remove anyone), then deletes the group. No errors.

### 3. Friends list
- **New "Friends" section** at the top of the 👥 panel. **+ add** takes a player name: a leading @ and capital letters are ignored, and unknown names get a clear message.
- **Requests need a yes:** "🤝 fay wants to be friends" with **Accept** or **No thanks**. Requests count toward the 👥 badge. If you add someone who already asked you, you simply become friends.
- **Friends list:** online friends come first, each with a green dot and what they're doing ("in a room", "on the menu"). Clicking an online friend opens their profile. **✕** removes a friend from both lists, after a "sure?".
- **Invite to your room:** while you wait in a room's lobby, online friends get an **invite** button.
  - They get a popup: "🎮 fay invited you to play Terminal Race! · **Join JPUB** · Not now". Join takes them straight into the room, leaving any room they're in.
  - Invites expire after 2 minutes, and only come from current friends who aren't blocked.
- **Accounts:** online, friends are signed-in accounts, because guests become a new player every visit. Guests see "Sign in to add friends and invite them to games." Practice rooms aren't offered, since they're on this computer only.
- **2 new trophies:** Best Buds (make a friend) and Party Starter (invite a friend to your room). Now 135 in total.
- **Verified:**

    - Friends lists and requests are private.
    - Room invites only between friends, only with real 4-letter room codes, and not while banned. Either friend can unfriend; outsiders can't.
    - Also friend sorting by online status (matched by account or tab), and invite expiry.

### 4. Kid safety: chat filter, reports, and a reports inbox for you
- **Chat filter (new `src/filter.js`)** in every chat: room chat, lobby chat, private chats and group chats.
  - **Rude words become `****`.** It also catches capital letters, look-alike numbers and symbols (1 for i, @ for a, $ for s), stretched letters ("soooo"), spaced-out spellings ("b a d") and mean phrases.
  - **Normal words are left alone:** class, cocktail, assembly, Dickens and night all pass.
  - **Personal info is hidden:** phone numbers become **[number hidden]** and emails become **[email hidden]**.
  - **Filtered when shown, not just when sent,** so a message from an old or modified copy of the game is still cleaned.
  - **Encoded word list:** stored encoded, so kids reading the code don't see the words. The file explains how to edit it.
- **Names:**
  - **Every player name** on screen is cleaned too (in the lobby, game, results and panels).
  - **Your own rude name** is swapped for "player" before anyone sees it, with a message asking for a kinder name.
  - **Refused outright:** rude account names and group names.
- **🚩 Report** button on every player's profile.
  - **The form:** pick a reason (mean or bullying, bad words, asking for personal info, cheating, rude name, something else) and add an optional note. If you were in a private chat with them, their last few messages are attached.
  - **Afterwards:** "Thanks for telling us", plus a one-click **Block them too**.
  - **Privacy and limits:** only the owner sees reports, and the reported player isn't told who sent it. One report every 30 seconds per player, enforced by the database rules, so nobody can flood your inbox.
- **Your reports inbox (owner only):** a red count on the 👑 button and a pop-up when a new report arrives.
  - In Owner tools → **Reports**: each report shows who, why, the note, who reported it and when.
  - **Ban 1 day** bans them straight from the report; **Dismiss** or **Done** clears it.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit tests (66 passing):**
    - **Filter:** catches the tricky spellings, leaves 10 normal phrases alone, hides phones and emails, and the source file contains no rude words.
    - **Report rules:** filing a report needs the rate-limit stamp; there's one every 30 seconds; nobody can report as someone else; only the listed reasons are accepted; notes are capped; and reports can't be overwritten. Banned players can't report. Only the owner can read or clear reports.
    - The rules simulator learned multi-path updates and a moving clock to test this.
  - **Browser test** with 3 tabs:
    - **Filter:** a rude word and a phone number in lobby chat arrive as `oh **** call me [number hidden]`. A rude name shows to others as "player", with a message to the player.
    - **Reporting:** Sam reports Tess for bad words with a note. An immediate second report is refused with "wait a little".
    - **Owner inbox:** the owner's 👑 shows "1" and the report lists Tess, Bad words, the note and Sam. Banning from the report works, then Dismiss clears it. Other players have no inbox. No errors.
- **Rules:** new `reports` and `reportsBy` sections (in the combined update at the bottom).
- **Test fix:** the Letter Lock browser check failed now and then. It looked from outside the page for the right letter on the line, so on a busy machine it could miss it, and it never confirmed a press worked. Now the page itself watches every frame for the letter, and each lock is confirmed (with a retry after a miss). The game itself was fine: the check's new diagnostics showed 58 to 60 frames per second and correct keyboard focus.

### 5. Chat emotes and quick-chat
- **Slash commands** in every chat (room, lobby, private and group):
  - `/me waves` shows as an action: ** emma waves*.
  - `/shrug` adds ¯\_(ツ)_/¯, `/gg` says "GG! 🎉 good game", `/roll` rolls 1–100 and `/flip` flips a coin.
  - `/help` lists them only for you, and disappears once you send your next message. A mistyped command explains itself instead of being sent.
- **Emoji picker:** a 😀 button next to the chat box opens 24 game-friendly emoji (🔥 👾 🔓 💻 🏆 and more) that insert where the cursor is. Typed smileys turn into emoji, such as :) → 🙂, <3 → ❤️ and :D → 😄. They only change as separate words, so links and code stay as typed.
- **Quick-chat buttons** above the room chat change with the chosen mode:
  - Each mode has 4 of its own plus "gl hf!" and "gg". Race has "Which layer are you on?", Duel has "Bring it! ⚡", Defend has "I'm being breached! 🚨", and quick hacks have "Which stage?".
  - Heist has "What do you see?" and **"Code is …"**, which puts the start of the sentence in the box for you to finish.
  - One click sends the rest. That's handy on a Chromebook mid-game.
- **Plain text underneath:** everything is ordinary text, so it needs **no database rule changes**, and the kid-safe filter still applies to all of it.
- **Verified:**
  - **Unit test (67 passing):** every command, including fixed dice and coin results; /help lists every command; only "/me " counts as an action; smileys convert without touching links; each mode's quick-chat list; at most 6 buttons.
  - **Browser test** with 2 tabs: /me, /roll and /help (seen only by the sender); emoji picked from the picker plus a smiley arrive as "🔥 so cool 🙂". Quick-chat buttons switch from Race to Heist when the host changes mode; "Code is …" fills the box and "gl hf!" sends in one click. No errors.
- **Test fix:** my new browser steps now skip the first-visit intro properly, using a pointer-down event like a real click (a scripted click didn't count).
- **Rules:** no change needed.
